Privacy Policy

OneFiles is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information such as your name, email address, and account details when you register. To provide our core utilities, we also request access to on-device data such as files, camera, microphone, and precise background location. We collect device info and usage data.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to operate and improve our services. Storage permissions are used strictly for local file management. Background location and SMS permissions are used exclusively by the AccidentAlert feature to notify your trusted contacts in emergencies. Chat prompts are processed to provide AI responses.

3. How We Safeguard Your Information

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. Your files, documents, and media are processed locally on your device and are not uploaded to our servers. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

4. Third-Party Services

We use Firebase for secure account synchronization and Unity for advertising. We also utilize third-party Cloud AI providers (such as Gemini/OpenAI) to process API Chat prompts. These services collect and process data in accordance with their own privacy policies.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
