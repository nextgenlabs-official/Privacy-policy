OneFiles · Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

OneFiles (“App”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) is an all-in-one file management and productivity application. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when you use the App.

The information processed depends on the features you use, the permissions you grant, your account settings, and the third-party services enabled in the App.

2. Information We Collect or Process

a) Account Information

When you sign in using Google Sign-In or email/password authentication, Firebase Authentication may process identifiers associated with your account, such as your email address, user ID, and, where provided, display name or profile photo. The information received depends on your authentication method and account permissions.

b) Diagnostics and Usage Information

Firebase Crashlytics may process crash reports, app state information, device model, operating system version, app version, and related diagnostic information. Other enabled services may process technical or usage information necessary to provide their features.

c) Advertising Information

Google AdMob and Unity LevelPlay, where enabled, may process advertising identifiers, IP address, device information, ad interactions, diagnostic information, and other data according to their SDK configurations, your consent and settings, and applicable policies. Personalised or non-personalised advertising availability depends on your region and the configuration of the services.

d) Referral and Subscription Information

The App may process referral codes, referral relationships, account identifiers, subscription status, and related records using Firebase Realtime Database or other configured services. Payment credentials are handled by the relevant payment provider and are not collected by us unless expressly disclosed.

e) Files and Documents

The App accesses files you select or make available through the permissions granted to it to support file management, media playback, PDF tools, document scanning, and related features.

Files processed entirely on your device are not automatically uploaded to our servers solely because the App accesses them. However, files or extracted content may be transmitted when you initiate a sharing or upload action or use a feature that requires remote processing, including cloud AI.

f) Location Information

Location permission may be used by features such as GPS Camera, Compass, or nearby-device discovery, depending on the feature and your device settings. Where a feature obtains precise or approximate location, it is processed as needed to provide that feature.

Location information may also be embedded in photos or other content if you enable a relevant feature. We do not claim that location is never transmitted; any transmission depends on the feature and your actions. We do not intentionally store location information on our own servers unless the relevant feature requires it and the collection is disclosed.

g) Camera and Microphone

Camera access may support document scanning, GPS Camera, and other camera-based tools. Microphone access may support screen recording, sound measurement, voice input, and AI voice features.

Where supported, processing may occur on-device. Audio, video, images, or transcripts may be transmitted to a remote service when you use a feature that requires it. The precise handling depends on the selected feature and processing mode.

h) Bluetooth and Nearby Devices

Bluetooth and nearby-device capabilities may be used for supported file sharing, device discovery, or audio features. Technical information may be exchanged with nearby devices or processed by the relevant platform service to establish a connection. The information handled depends on the feature and the underlying technology.

i) Notifications and Firebase Cloud Messaging

If push notifications are enabled, Firebase Cloud Messaging may process a device registration token and related delivery information. Tokens may be associated with your account or device in our configured systems to deliver notifications such as reminders, verses, or app updates.

You can manage notification permissions through your device settings. Disabling notifications may prevent delivery of certain messages.

j) AI Queries and Voice Content

In local AI mode, supported AI processing occurs on your device. In cloud AI mode, prompts, extracted document text, voice transcripts, or other content you submit may be sent to the relevant AI provider for processing.

The provider may process technical request information according to its own terms and privacy policy. Data retention, model-improvement practices, and other processing depend on the provider and service configuration. We will identify relevant providers in the App or accompanying disclosures where applicable.

Avoid submitting passwords, payment credentials, confidential documents, or other sensitive information unless necessary and you understand the applicable processing arrangements.

k) Network and Peer-to-Peer Information

File-sharing, torrent, and chat features may process IP addresses, connection information, device identifiers used by the relevant technology, and information necessary to establish or maintain connections. Torrent peers or trackers may be able to see your public IP address and torrent-related connection information.

The exact information exchanged depends on the protocol, network configuration, and feature you use.

3. How We Use Information

We may use information, as applicable, to:

  • Provide and maintain app features.
  • Authenticate users and manage account access.
  • Operate referral and subscription features.
  • Deliver notifications requested or enabled by users.
  • Display and measure advertisements.
  • Diagnose crashes, improve performance, and troubleshoot issues.
  • Respond to support requests and protect the security of the App.
  • Process cloud AI requests and other user-requested online features.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms.

4. Third-Party Services and Data Sharing

Depending on the features enabled, the App may use:

  • Firebase Authentication — account authentication.
  • Firebase Realtime Database and other configured Firebase services — referral records, account-related data, or application functionality.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash diagnostics.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging — push notifications.
  • Firebase In-App Messaging — in-app messages, if enabled.
  • Google AdMob — advertising.
  • Unity LevelPlay — advertising mediation.
  • Google Play Billing — purchases and subscriptions.
  • Google Nearby Connections — supported nearby-device communication.
  • WebRTC or a hosted communication provider — real-time chat or communication, where enabled.
  • jlibtorrent or related torrent components — torrent functionality.
  • Cloud AI providers — AI requests, if cloud AI is enabled.

The services listed above may process information according to their technical role, configuration, and applicable privacy terms. Not every service necessarily receives personal information in every use of the App.

We may also disclose information where required by law, to respond to valid legal requests, or to protect the security, rights, and safety of users and others.

5. Data Sales and Advertising

We do not sell personal information for money. However, advertising and analytics services may process or disclose information for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, or other purposes described in their policies. Depending on applicable law, certain advertising-related disclosures may be treated as sharing or targeted advertising.

Where required, we will provide appropriate consent and privacy controls. Available advertising settings may also be managed through your device or the relevant service.

6. Data Storage and International Processing

Information may be stored on your device or processed by third-party providers on servers located in India or other countries, depending on the service and its configuration.

The location and safeguards applicable to a particular service depend on the provider's arrangements. We take reasonable steps appropriate to our role to protect information and select services.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, subject to applicable legal, security, and operational requirements.

Account information may be retained while your account remains active. Diagnostic information may be retained according to the configured retention settings of the relevant service. Referral, transaction, security, and legal records may be retained for a longer period where justified.

Actual retention periods depend on the service and configuration. Where we control the information, we take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify it when it is no longer required. Third-party providers may retain information under their own policies.

8. Account and Data Deletion

Users can initiate account deletion through the Delete Account option available within the OneFiles app.

When an account deletion request is completed, we will delete the personal information associated with the account that we control, subject to applicable legal obligations and legitimate retention requirements.

Uninstalling OneFiles does not automatically delete your account or information stored by online services.
