Privacy Policy for Space Shooter: Neon Void

Application ID: com.neonvoid.game

Last Updated: August 27, 2026

Thank you for playing Space Shooter: Neon Void ("the Game", "we", "us", or "our"), developed by NextGen Labs™. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you download, install, and play our game. By accessing or using Space Shooter: Neon Void, you acknowledge and agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

NextGen Labs™ does not directly collect, store, or transmit personally identifiable information (PII) such as your real name, physical address, email address, or phone number. All gameplay progression, high scores, hangar upgrades, and banked cogs are stored locally on your device storage.

However, to provide gameplay services and deliver rewarded advertisements, third-party software development kits (SDKs) integrated into the app may automatically collect certain diagnostic and non-personal data:

  • Device Information: Device model, manufacturer, hardware specifications, operating system version, and system language.
  • Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID / AD_ID) used for delivering personalized and rewarded video ads.
  • Network & Connection Data: IP address, mobile network status, and carrier connection information.
  • Diagnostic & Performance Data: Application load times, crash logs, frame metrics, and stability diagnostics via Android Vitals.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Space Shooter: Neon Void is free-to-play and supported by third-party advertising networks. These networks may collect and use pseudonymous device identifiers to serve video advertisements, measure ad efficacy, and offer optional rewarded revives or ad-free passes.

The third-party providers integrated into the Game include:

  • Unity Ads (Unity Technologies): Used to deliver video ads and rewarded revive gameplay mechanics.
    Privacy Policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Google Play Services: Used for platform distribution, security integrity, and Google Play Store services.
    Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. Permissions Used

Space Shooter: Neon Void requests only essential system permissions required for gameplay connectivity:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Allows the game to connect to third-party ad networks to load rewarded revive videos.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Enables the app to verify network availability before attempting to request ads.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID: Allows third-party ad partners (Unity Ads) to access the standard Android Advertising ID to provide relevant video ads in accordance with Google Play Developer Policies.

4. Managing Advertising Preferences & Opt-Out

You have full control over how advertising identifiers are used on your Android device:

  • Go to your device's Settings ➔ Google ➔ Ads.
  • Tap Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID to opt-out of personalized interest-based advertising.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Space Shooter: Neon Void is designed for general audiences and is not directed to children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in certain European jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information without consent, please contact us immediately, and we will take prompt steps to delete any such data.

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and procedural safeguards to ensure data processed by our third-party SDK partners is handled securely. However, please be aware that no transmission over the internet or electronic storage solution can be guaranteed 100% secure.
