Privacy Policy for Neon Blocks

Application ID: com.neonblocks.game • Last Updated: 5 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Neon Blocks ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles user information. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding any data collected while you play our game.

Core Summary: Neon Blocks does not require any account creation, login, or personal profile. We do not collect your name, email address, physical location, or contact list.

1. Information Collected Automatically

To provide arcade gameplay, display rewarded/interstitial advertisements, and monitor game stability, third-party advertising SDKs integrated into the app may automatically collect certain device and technical information, including:

  • Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID / AD_ID) to deliver personalized or frequency-capped advertisements.
  • Device Information: Device model, manufacturer, operating system version, screen resolution, and language settings.
  • Network & Connection: IP address (used to infer broad non-precise geographic region like country or city) and network connection type (Wi-Fi or cellular).
  • Gameplay & Ad Interaction Data: Information regarding ad impressions, ad clicks, video completion, and crash diagnostic logs.

2. Third-Party Service Providers

We partner with trusted third-party monetization and service providers that process data strictly in accordance with their respective privacy policies:

  • Unity Ads (Unity Technologies): Used to deliver video interstitials and rewarded ads (e.g., revive chances and ad-free passes).
    Unity Privacy Policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Google Play Services: Used for core Android platform integration and advertising identifier compliance.
    Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. How We Use Collected Information

The information collected through third-party SDKs is used solely for:

  • Delivering rewarded ads (such as player revives and 1-hour ad-free passes) and throttled interstitial ads.
  • Preventing invalid clicks and fraudulent ad impressions.
  • Ad frequency capping (e.g., ensuring interstitial ads adhere to our 10-minute cooldown).
  • Monitoring app performance, stability, and fixing crashes.

4. Permissions Requested by the App

The App requests only standard, non-sensitive Android permissions necessary for gameplay:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required to load ads and check connectivity.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to detect whether internet is available.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Required to provide tactile haptic vibration feedback during block rotations and line clears.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID: Standard Google Play permission required on Android 13+ to provide advertising identifier capabilities to ad SDKs.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Families Policy)

Neon Blocks is intended for general audiences aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child under 13 has provided personal data through the App, please contact us so we can take immediate steps to remove such information.

6. Your Privacy Choices & Opt-Out Options

You have full control over advertising identifiers on your Android device:

  • Reset or Delete Advertising ID: On your Android device, navigate to Settings → Google → Ads where you can reset your Advertising ID or delete it entirely.
  • Opt Out of Personalized Ads: You can opt out of interest-based ads under your Android Google settings or visit https://www.aboutads.info/choices/.
  • Ad-Free Gameplay: You can unlock 1-hour ad-free gameplay passes directly inside the game by watching a rewarded video.

7. Data Security

All network transmissions conducted by third-party SDKs utilize industry-standard Transport Layer Security (HTTPS) encryption. High scores and game settings are stored locally on your device via Android SharedPreferences and are never sent to external servers.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update our Privacy Policy to reflect changes in legal requirements, store policies, or app functionality. Any modifications will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
