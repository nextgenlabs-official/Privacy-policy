Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to One4Me - All in One (“One4Me”, “App”, “we”, “us”, or “our”). One4Me is a versatile mobile application combining an everyday files organizer, spatial 8D audio music player, cinema theatre video player, private web browser, casual gaming hub, AI assistant, and 50+ smart productivity tools.

We respect your privacy and believe user data protection is a fundamental right. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App accesses, how it is handled, and how your information is kept secure when using One4Me.

2. Google Play Data Safety Summary

For transparency and alignment with Google Play’s Data Safety standards:

  • No Personal Media or Document Collection: We do NOT collect, upload, index, or sell your local files, photos, videos, documents, or audio recordings.
  • Data Encrypted in Transit: All communication (account authentication, cloud sync, and diagnostic crash reports) is encrypted using industry-standard HTTPS/TLS.
  • Data Deletion Supported: You can delete your account and all associated cloud data at any time directly in the App (Settings > Account > Delete Account) or by emailing us.

3. Information We Access, Process, or Store

a) Files Organizer, Documents, and Local Media Storage

The App accesses storage solely to provide on-device file management, spatial audio playback, cinema video playback, document viewing, and media utilities:

  • Scoped Storage Compliance: On Android 13 and above, the App requests granular Android MediaStore permissions (READ_MEDIA_AUDIO, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_MEDIA_IMAGES) and Android 14+ partial media selection (READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED). Standard storage permissions (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) are used strictly on Android 12 and below.
  • 100% On-Device File Processing: All file management operations—including browsing folders, sorting downloads, viewing documents/PDFs, compressing/extracting ZIP archives, and viewing storage statistics—are executed completely on your device. We do NOT upload, monitor, analyze, or back up your personal files to our servers.
  • Trash & Storage Cleaner: The storage cleaning utility identifies and clears temporary cache files, thumbnails, and residual app data on-device. It never accesses or deletes your personal documents, photos, or media without your explicit command.
  • User-Initiated Sharing: Your files are only shared when you explicitly initiate an action (e.g., sharing a file via third-party apps, transferring via offline P2P Flash Share, or voluntarily submitting text to Darling AI).

b) Account Information (Optional)

If you choose to sign in using Google Sign-In or email authentication, Firebase Authentication securely processes your email address, unique user identifier, and profile information. Authentication is optional for basic local utilities.

c) Diagnostics and Crash Reporting

To identify bugs and maintain app stability, Firebase Crashlytics processes anonymized crash logs, device hardware models, OS versions, and operational state at the time of an error. This diagnostic data is strictly non-personally identifiable.

d) Advertising and Mediation

To support free features, the App integrates Google AdMob and Unity LevelPlay mediation. These advertising SDKs may process advertising identifiers (GAID), IP addresses, and interaction metrics to serve relevant, policy-compliant ads according to your regional consent preferences.

e) Camera, Sensors, and Hardware Utilities

Specific micro-tools utilize device hardware strictly when opened:

  • Camera: Used exclusively for Document Scanning and GPS Map Camera geotagging. Camera feeds are processed entirely in real-time on-device and are never recorded or transmitted without your explicit command.
  • Microphone: Used locally for the Decibel Sound Meter, Voice Notes, and Screen Recording. Audio data is processed on-device.
  • Sensors & Compass: Magnetic and orientation sensors are read locally for the Compass, Ruler, and EMF Detector tools. Sensor readings are never transmitted.

f) Location Information (Foreground Only)

Location permissions (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) are requested strictly in the foreground for features that inherently depend on location, such as the GPS Map Camera, Compass, and Speedometer. Location data is never tracked, monitored, or collected in the background.

g) Bluetooth & Nearby Devices

Permissions for nearby devices (BLUETOOTH_CONNECT, BLUETOOTH_SCAN, NEARBY_WIFI_DEVICES) are used exclusively for offline P2P file transfers (Flash Share) and wireless Bluetooth Audio Sharing. These permissions are flagged with neverForLocation and are never used to track your physical location.

h) Push Notifications & Timed Reminders

With your explicit permission, Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) processes a temporary push token to deliver updates. Exact alarm permissions (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM) are used strictly to trigger your user-scheduled medicine reminders, custom alerts, and daily planner tasks at the exact scheduled time.

i) AI Features (Darling AI)

In local AI mode, queries are executed completely offline on your device. When using cloud AI mode, the prompt or text you voluntarily submit is transmitted to the AI provider to generate a response. Avoid submitting passwords or sensitive financial data in AI prompts.

5. How We Use Information

We process information only for lawful, transparent purposes, including:

  • Delivering core files organizer, media playback, and utility features.
  • Authenticating user accounts and managing referral/subscription status.
  • Delivering user-scheduled reminders and notifications.
  • Diagnosing application crashes and improving performance.
  • Displaying compliant advertisements via authorized advertising partners.
  • Preventing fraud, safeguarding app security, and complying with legal obligations.

6. Third-Party Service Providers

One4Me works with reputable third-party services that may process technical data in compliance with applicable privacy regulations:

  • Google Play Services: Core platform support, in-app updates, and Nearby Connections.
  • Firebase (Authentication, Crashlytics, Cloud Messaging): Account security, crash analytics, and notifications.
  • Google AdMob & Unity LevelPlay: In-app advertising and ad mediation.
  • Google Play Billing: Secure purchase and subscription processing (payment details are handled entirely by Google).

7. Data Retention and Security

We implement standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS) and modern platform security standards. Because your files, photos, and music remain stored locally on your device, you retain complete control over your content at all times. Uninstalling the app removes all app-specific caches.

8. Account and Data Deletion

You can delete your account and associated cloud records at any time:

  • In-App: Navigate to Settings > Account > Delete Account.
  • By Email: Send an account deletion request to official.nextgenlabs@gmail.com with your registered email.

Upon confirmation, your authentication records and cloud database entries will be permanently deleted from our servers within 30 days.

One4Me is intended for general audiences and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe personal data of a child has been collected, please contact us immediately.
