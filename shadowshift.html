Privacy Policy for Shadow Shift

Effective Date: September 5, 2026 • Application Package: com.shadowshift.game

This Privacy Policy describes how Shadow Shift ("we", "our", or "the Game") handles your information when you download, install, and play our mobile game.

We do not collect, transmit, store, track, or share any personal information, device identifiers, location data, or gameplay analytics with anyone.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

We respect your digital privacy. When you play Shadow Shift:

  • No Account Creation: You do not need to register, create a username, provide an email address, or log in with social media.
  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not ask for, capture, or record your name, contact details, phone number, or physical address.
  • No Financial or Payment Data: The Game contains zero paywalls, microtransactions, subscriptions, or digital store purchases. No billing data is processed.
  • No Network Access: Shadow Shift does not request the android.permission.INTERNET permission. The application cannot send or receive data across the internet.

2. Local Device Storage (Offline Progress)

To preserve your gameplay achievements between game sessions, the Game saves gameplay state strictly in private local storage on your device (using standard Android local preferences).

This local data includes only:

  • Level progress and highest checkpoint reached (Checkpoints 1 through 50)
  • Total recorded death counters
  • Best speedrun finish times
  • User settings (sound effects volume, background music volume, haptic vibration toggle, touch control sizing, and left-handed orientation layout)

Your Data Stays On Your Phone: This information is stored exclusively on your device sandbox and is never transmitted to external cloud servers, databases, or third parties. If you clear the application cache/data or uninstall the Game, this local data is permanently removed.

3. Device Hardware Permissions

Shadow Shift requests the minimum necessary permissions to function:

Permission Technical Identifier Purpose & Scope
Haptic Vibration android.permission.VIBRATE Provides tactile feedback when the player interacts with game elements, triggers traps, or respawns at checkpoints. Can be disabled at any time in the in-game Settings menu.

The Game does NOT request sensitive permissions such as Location, Camera, Microphone, Read/Write External Storage, Contacts, Phone State, or Body Sensors.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertisements

Unlike many mobile games, Shadow Shift contains zero third-party advertising SDKs and zero analytics platforms:

  • No Ad Networks: We do not use Google AdMob, Unity Ads, AppLovin, IronSource, or any ad trackers.
  • No Advertising ID (GAID / AAID): We do not collect, access, or track the Google Advertising Identifier.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: We do not integrate Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, Adjust, AppsFlyer, or Facebook SDK.
  • No External Links: The game does not embed external web links or external browsers.

5. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families Policy)

Protecting young players is of paramount importance to us:

  • Shadow Shift is designed for a general audience of all ages, including children.
  • Because the Game does not collect, request, or share any personal information or device identifiers, it is fully compliant with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the Google Play Families Policy.
  • Parents can rest assured that children can play the Game in a completely safe, tracker-free, and ad-free offline environment.

6. European & California Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Under data privacy frameworks such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA):

  • Data Minimization: We do not process or control personal data of any kind.
  • No "Sale" or "Sharing" of Data: We do not sell, rent, or share personal or behavioral data.
  • Right to Deletion: Because all data is stored exclusively on your device, you can exercise full control and deletion of all saved data at any time by clearing the Game's data in Android Settings > Apps > Shadow Shift > Storage > Clear Storage, or by uninstalling the application.

7. Security of Your Information

Since we do not transmit or host your data on any remote servers, there is no risk of your personal data being intercepted or compromised via our services. All local gameplay data is isolated within Android's secure application sandbox.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may occasionally update our Privacy Policy to reflect future game enhancements or updates to Google Play policies. Any updates will be published on this page with an updated "Effective Date".
