Privacy Policy

App: Color Dino: Pixel Run (Package: com.colordino.game)

Last Updated: September 5, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Color Dino: Pixel Run ("we", "our", or "the Game") handles user information. We are committed to protecting your privacy and providing a safe, enjoyable gaming experience.

Summary: Color Dino does NOT collect, store, or require personal identifiable information (such as your name, email, address, or phone number). The game operates locally on your device, with optional ads provided by Unity Technologies.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect:

  • Personal identities, accounts, or login credentials
  • Location data (GPS)
  • Contact lists or camera/microphone recordings
  • Financial details or credit card information

3. Third-Party Advertising (Unity Ads)

To support ongoing development and offer optional in-game rewards (such as revives and temporary ad-free passes), the game integrates the Unity Ads SDK provided by Unity Technologies.

Unity Ads may collect and process device telemetry and identifiers, strictly for ad delivery, fraud prevention, and frequency capping:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (AD_ID), Android ID, device make and model.
  • Network Information: IP address, mobile network carrier, and connection type.
  • Ad Interaction Data: Impressions, video completions, and clicks.

For more details on how Unity processes data, please visit the Unity Game Player Privacy Policy.

4. Local Data Storage

The game saves gameplay state (such as your high score and ad-free buff timestamp) locally on your device via HTML5 LocalStorage and Android SharedPreferences. This data remains on your device and is never uploaded to any external servers.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Color Dino is designed for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to third-party ad networks through our app, please contact us immediately so we can investigate and address it.

6. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your location, you have rights regarding your advertising identifiers:

  • Reset Advertising ID: You can reset or delete your Google Advertising ID at any time via Settings > Google > Ads > Reset/Delete Advertising ID.
  • Opt Out of Personalized Ads: You can opt out of interest-based ads via your Android device settings.

7. Security

We implement industry-standard practices and Android platform security sandboxing to protect the integrity of the application. All network communications made by the ad SDK use encrypted HTTPS connections.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions, feedback, or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via official.nextgenlabs@gmail.com.
