Devil Dash Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game application Devil Dash (referred to as "the Game", "we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting and respecting your privacy.

1. Information We Collect

Devil Dash does not require user accounts or collect personally identifiable information (such as your name, physical address, or phone number).

We may automatically receive certain non-personal diagnostic and technical data through third-party services:

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, screen resolution, and language settings.
  • Gameplay & Progress Data: High scores, unlocked levels, and death counters stored locally on your device storage.
  • Diagnostic Data: Crash reports and performance metrics to help us optimize game frame pacing and fix bugs.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

The Game integrates third-party SDKs to provide rewarded advertising and engine functionality. These third-party providers may collect anonymized device identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID, IP address) in accordance with their privacy policies:

  • Unity Ads (Unity Technologies): Used to deliver video advertisements. For more information, please review the Unity Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Services: Used for application distribution and platform support. For more details, see the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your level progress, custom control layouts, and audio settings are saved locally on your device using standard Android application storage. We do not transmit or sell your personal game data to third parties.

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA)

Our Game is designed for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will take prompt steps to delete such data.

5. User Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the right to:

  • Opt out of personalized advertising via your device settings (e.g., Google Settings > Ads > Reset Advertising ID / Opt out of Ads Personalization).
  • Delete all locally stored game progress at any time by clearing the application's data via your Android device settings.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
